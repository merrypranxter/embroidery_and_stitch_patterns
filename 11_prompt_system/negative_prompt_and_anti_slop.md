# Negative Prompt And Anti Slop

Avoid: flat print, vector-only, painted texture, plastic thread, random yarn mess, generic floral wallpaper, fake embroidery texture without stitches, impossible microscopic detail, cultural mishmash, unreadable stitch soup.

Use corrective phrases: `visible individual stitches`, `thread passes through fabric`, `raised fiber relief`, `cloth grain`, `stitched not printed`, `tension variation`, `macro textile surface`.
