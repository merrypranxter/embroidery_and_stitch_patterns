# Stitch Vs Motif

A motif is the subject or shape. A stitch is the method of making it. The same flower changes completely when rendered as satin stitch, chain stitch, long-and-short shading, French knots, stumpwork petals, or blackwork fill.

## Useful Questions

- What stitch family is doing the visual work?
- Is the motif freehand, counted, gridded, or digitized?
- Is the effect flat, raised, open, dense, shiny, matte, or dimensional?
- How does thread direction change the light?

## Prompt Language

Use phrases like `visible needle path`, `raised thread ridges`, `directional stitch sheen`, `counted fabric grid`, `tactile surface embroidery`, `not printed, actually stitched`, and `thread density follows the form`.
