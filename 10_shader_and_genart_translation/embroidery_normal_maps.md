# Embroidery Normal Maps

Normal maps can fake raised thread by creating ridges along stitch paths. Satin stitch needs parallel ridges; knots need radial bumps; couching needs cord-like cylinders.

## Controls

- stitch_length
- stitch_spacing
- thread_width
- height_strength
- jitter_amount
- fabric_noise
- color_palette
- curve_following

## Output Goal

The result should look like thread placed on cloth, not generic stripes or noise.
