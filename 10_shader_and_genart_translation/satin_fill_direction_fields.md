# Satin Fill Direction Fields

Satin fill can be modeled as a region filled with parallel thread strokes. Direction should follow local form, contour, or manually defined angle fields.

## Controls

- stitch_length
- stitch_spacing
- thread_width
- height_strength
- jitter_amount
- fabric_noise
- color_palette
- curve_following

## Output Goal

The result should look like thread placed on cloth, not generic stripes or noise.
