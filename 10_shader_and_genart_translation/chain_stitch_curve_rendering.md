# Chain Stitch Curve Rendering

Chain stitch curves are made of repeated loop glyphs aligned along a path. Overlap and tangent direction sell the looped structure.

## Controls

- stitch_length
- stitch_spacing
- thread_width
- height_strength
- jitter_amount
- fabric_noise
- color_palette
- curve_following

## Output Goal

The result should look like thread placed on cloth, not generic stripes or noise.
