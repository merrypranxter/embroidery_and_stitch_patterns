# Knot Particle Fields

Knots can be rendered as raised circular particles with slight random size and shadow. Great for pollen, stars, grit, and bead fields.

## Controls

- stitch_length
- stitch_spacing
- thread_width
- height_strength
- jitter_amount
- fabric_noise
- color_palette
- curve_following

## Output Goal

The result should look like thread placed on cloth, not generic stripes or noise.
