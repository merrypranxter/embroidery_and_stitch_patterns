# Bad Vs Good Examples

Bad: `floral embroidery pattern` with no stitch types, no fabric ground, no thread behavior.

Good: `floral surface embroidery on linen using satin stitch petals, stem stitch vines, French knot centers, raised cotton floss, visible cloth grain, directional thread sheen`.

Bad: random cultural mashup.

Good: respectful structural note: `counted geometric cross-stitch border with regional motif research references, not claiming authenticity`.
