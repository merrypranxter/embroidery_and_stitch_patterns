# Goldwork

Goldwork uses metallic threads, purls, passing thread, chips, and couching. The thread is often too precious or stiff to pierce the cloth repeatedly, so it is laid and secured.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`goldwork, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
