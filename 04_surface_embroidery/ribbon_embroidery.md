# Ribbon Embroidery

Ribbon embroidery uses silk or synthetic ribbon to form plush petals, leaves, bows, and dimensional floral forms.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`ribbon embroidery, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
