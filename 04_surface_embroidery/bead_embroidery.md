# Bead Embroidery

Bead embroidery adds seed beads, sequins, bugle beads, cabochons, and raised sparkle architecture onto cloth.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`bead embroidery, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
