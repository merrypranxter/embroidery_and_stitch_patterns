# Sequins And Spangles

Sequins are attached as reflective discs, producing point highlights, overlap shimmer, and scale-like surface effects.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`sequins and spangles, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
