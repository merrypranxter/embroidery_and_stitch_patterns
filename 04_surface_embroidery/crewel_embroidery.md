# Crewel Embroidery

Crewel embroidery uses wool thread, often on linen or twill, with rich botanical, animal, and scrolling motifs. It has a soft matte raised quality and loves Jacobean weird flowers.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`crewel embroidery, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
