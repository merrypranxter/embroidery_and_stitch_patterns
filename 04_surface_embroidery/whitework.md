# Whitework

Whitework uses white thread on white fabric, relying on texture, shadow, holes, pulled threads, and relief rather than color contrast.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`whitework, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
