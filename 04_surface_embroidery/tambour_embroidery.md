# Tambour Embroidery

Tambour uses a hooked needle to make rapid chain stitches, often for couture beadwork and decorative lines.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`tambour embroidery, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
