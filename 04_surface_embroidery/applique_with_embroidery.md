# Applique With Embroidery

Applique attaches fabric shapes to a ground and uses embroidery to secure, outline, decorate, or integrate them.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`applique with embroidery, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
