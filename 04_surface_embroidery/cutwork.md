# Cutwork

Cutwork removes fabric areas and secures edges with buttonhole or satin stitches. The design lives in both thread and holes.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`cutwork, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
