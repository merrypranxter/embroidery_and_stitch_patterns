# Smocking

Smocking gathers fabric into elastic pleats and decorates or stabilizes those pleats with stitches. It is structure and ornament at once.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`smocking, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
