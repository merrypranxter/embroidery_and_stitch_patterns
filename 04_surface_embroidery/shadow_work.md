# Shadow Work

Shadow work is stitched from the reverse side on sheer fabric, creating soft colored shadows visible through the ground.

## Visual Traits

- visible raised surface
- strong material behavior
- stitch direction matters
- shadows appear at thread edges
- irregularities make it feel real

## Prompt Module

`shadow work, tactile embroidery texture, raised thread, cloth ground, material-specific stitching, macro textile detail`
